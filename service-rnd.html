From Concept to Functional Hardware: Accelerated R&D and Rapid Prototyping

The most difficult distance to bridge is the gap between a schematic on a screen and a functional device in the hand. We specialize in that crossing.

Our Technical Workflow

  • 1. Requirements Engineering & Architecture: Defining technical specifications, power budgets, and system architecture to ensure all design goals are met before the first trace is drawn.
  • 2. Rapid Proof-of-Concept (PoC): Initial functional validation using off-the-shelf modules and breadboarding to confirm the core logic and feasibility of the design.
  • 3. Schematic Design & Component Selection: Development of custom schematics with a focus on sourcing long-lifecycle components and optimizing the Bill of Materials (BOM).
  • 4. Custom Firmware Development: Low-level driver development and application logic for ARM, AVR, and ESP32 platforms, focusing on real-time performance and low power consumption.
  • 5. Multi-Layer PCB Prototyping: Engineering high-density, multi-layer prototypes designed for signal integrity and thermal stability from the very first revision.
  • 6. 3D Mechanical Integration & Enclosure Design: Co-designing the electronics alongside 3D CAD models to ensure perfect fitment within custom housings and optimized heat dissipation.
  • 7. Hardware-in-the-Loop (HiL) Testing: Simulated environment testing to verify firmware behavior against edge cases and hardware constraints before field deployment.
  • 8. Environmental & Stress Validation: Subjecting prototypes to thermal cycling, vibration, and load testing to identify potential failure points in demanding environments.
  • 9. Design for Excellence (DFX) Optimization: Refining the prototype for manufacturability (DFM) and assembly (DFA) to reduce unit costs and increase production yields.
  • 10. Regulatory Pre-Compliance Screening: Internal testing for EMI/EMC and safety standards to ensure the final product is ready for formal FCC, CE, or BIS certification.

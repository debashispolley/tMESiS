About Us

"tMESiS is more than an electronics provider; we are a dedicated engineering collective committed to solving the most complex hardware challenges of the modern era. Founded with the vision of streamlining the electronic product lifecycle, tMESiS has evolved into a multi-disciplinary hub where technical theory meets industrial reality. Based in the heart of Kolkata’s growing tech ecosystem, we serve a diverse clientele—from independent inventors and high-growth startups to established industrial enterprises. We understand that in the world of electronics, a single millimetre of trace or a single micro-ohm of resistance can be the difference between success and failure. That is why we treat every project with the same level of rigorous engineering scrutiny."

Our Vision

To lead the digital transformation of the hardware industry in India and beyond, setting the standard for integrity, precision, and engineering innovation.

Our Mission

To deliver industrial-grade engineering solutions through meticulous PCB design, rigorous validation, and the sourcing of authorized electronic hardware. We strive to be the most trusted partner in our clients' journey from prototype to production.

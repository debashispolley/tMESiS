Setup Your Workbench

In engineering, what cannot be measured cannot be improved. We provide the high-precision instruments that turn invisible signals into actionable data.

Available Equipment

  • 1. Digital Storage Oscilloscopes (DSO): Multi-channel, high-bandwidth oscilloscopes with deep memory and serial bus decoding (I2C, SPI, CAN) for real-time waveform analysis.
  • 2. Programmable DC Power Supplies: High-precision linear and switching DC supplies with over-voltage protection (OVP) and low-ripple output for stable hardware testing.
  • 3. Precision Digital Multimeters (DMM): Benchtop and handheld 4.5 to 6.5 digit multimeters for high-resolution voltage, current, resistance, and capacitance testing.
  • 4. Spectrum & Network Analyzers: Advanced tools for EMI troubleshooting, frequency response characterization, and antenna tuning in the GHz range.
  • 5. ESD-Safe Soldering & Rework Stations: Temperature-controlled soldering irons and hot-air rework stations with rapid thermal recovery for SMD and through-hole components.
  • 6. Logic Analyzers: High-speed multi-channel logic analyzers for capturing and visualizing digital timing diagrams and state-machine transitions.
  • 7. Arbitrary Function Generators: Dual-channel signal generators capable of producing sine, square, pulse, and custom arbitrary waveforms for circuit stress testing.
  • 8. Electronic DC Loads: Programmable electronic loads for discharging batteries and testing the efficiency of DC-DC converters under various load conditions.
  • 9. Stereo Zoom Microscopes: Trinocular microscopes with high-magnification optics and LED ring lighting for inspecting 0201 components and solder joint integrity.
  • 10. Thermal Imaging Cameras: High-resolution radiometric thermal imagers to visualize heat dissipation and identify failing components during long-duration load tests.

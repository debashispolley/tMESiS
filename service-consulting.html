Supply Chain & Engineering Advice

In hardware, a single obsolete component can halt an entire production line. We provide the foresight to keep your supply chain resilient and your designs future-proof.

Services Provided

  • 1. BOM Scrubbing & Lifecycle Analysis: Comprehensive audit of your Bill of Materials to identify End-of-Life (EOL) or 'Not Recommended for New Design' (NRND) components before they impact production.
  • 2. Design for Manufacturability (DFM) Review: Pre-fabrication analysis to ensure your layout aligns with global PCB house capabilities, reducing board house queries and fabrication delays.
  • 3. Component Cross-Referencing: Identifying pin-compatible, cost-effective alternatives for high-lead-time parts to keep your production lines moving during global supply shortages.
  • 4. Technical Feasibility Studies: In-depth evaluation of your product concept’s power requirements, thermal constraints, and physical size to determine project viability before investment.
  • 5. Vendor Selection & Supply Chain Audit: Leveraging our network of authorized distributors and fabricators to help you select the right partners for your specific volume and quality requirements.
  • 6. Value Engineering & Cost Reduction: Analyzing existing designs to simplify circuitry and consolidate components, reducing the total unit cost without sacrificing performance.
  • 7. Regulatory Roadmap & Compliance Strategy: Consultation on global certification requirements (FCC, CE, BIS, RoHS) to ensure your product is designed for compliance from day one.
  • 8. Root Cause Analysis (RCA) for Hardware Failures: Diagnostic consulting to identify why existing hardware is failing in the field, providing actionable engineering fixes for EMI, thermal, or logic issues.
  • 9. Manufacturing Transfer Support: Assistance in moving from in-house prototyping to external Contract Manufacturing (CM), including the creation of detailed assembly documentation.
  • 10. IoT & Wireless Strategy: Expert advice on selecting the right wireless protocol (LoRa, Wi-Fi, BLE, or Cellular) based on range, power consumption, and data throughput needs.

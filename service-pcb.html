High-Speed Performance by Design: Where Signal Integrity Meets Robust Power Architecture

In high-speed design, every trace is an antenna and every plane is a capacitor. We don't just route boards; we manage the physics of information.

Our Capabilities

  • 1. High-Speed Signal Integrity (SI): Impedance-controlled routing and differential pair matching for high-speed interfaces like DDR4/5, PCIe Gen 4, and USB 4.0.
  • 2. Power Integrity (PI) Optimization: Comprehensive PDN (Power Delivery Network) analysis to minimize IR drop, optimize decoupling capacitor placement, and reduce plane resonance.
  • 3. Multi-Layer Stackup Management: Strategic stackup design for complex 4 to 16+ layer boards, utilizing blind and buried vias to maximize routing density and minimize EMI.
  • 4. EMI/EMC Compliant Layout: Radiation-aware layout techniques designed to pass FCC/CE certification on the first spin, including guard traces and Faraday shielding.
  • 5. Advanced DFM & DFA Analysis: Rigorous Design-for-Manufacturing checks to eliminate fabrication bottlenecks and reduce assembly yield loss.
  • 6. Mixed-Signal Isolation: Precision partitioning of sensitive analog circuits and high-noise digital domains to maintain a low noise floor and high SNR.
  • 7. Thermal Management & Heat Dissipation: Integration of thermal vias, heavy copper pours (up to 4oz), and metal-core substrates for high-power LED or motor driver applications.
  • 8. RF & Antenna Integration: Layout and simulation of 2.4GHz/5GHz RF circuits, including onboard inverted-F or patch antenna tuning and coplanar waveguide routing.
  • 9. Rigid-Flex and Flexible PCB Design: Design of complex rigid-flex transitions for space-constrained enclosures, ensuring mechanical durability in dynamic-flex applications.
  • 10. Post-Layout Simulation & Verification: End-to-end verification using industry-standard tools to simulate crosstalk, eye diagrams, and thermal gradients before manufacturing.
